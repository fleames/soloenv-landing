The problem

Production is not a staging environment.

Solo developers building web apps with Docker often skip staging entirely. The first place a feature meets the real world is production — and your users.

When you do try to stage, the blockers are always the same: cost, time, and complexity. Wiring hosts, secrets, and teardown before you even have paying users is a detour you can't afford.

How it works

Three steps. No DevOps detour.

  1. 1

    Run one command

    From your project folder, run soloenv up. It uses the Docker workflow you already have — compose file or Dockerfile.

  2. 2

    Get a shareable URL

    SoloEnv starts your app and opens a secure tunnel, then prints a public https:// link you can send to anyone.

  3. 3

    Tear it down

    Done reviewing? soloenv down stops everything and cleans up. Nothing left running, nothing to pay for.

FAQ

Questions, answered.

Is SoloEnv live yet?

Not yet. This page is part of a 2-week validation experiment. If enough solo devs want it, we build the MVP.

What happens when I join the waitlist?

You complete a short Google Form (email plus optional role and stage). We may invite a small group for early manual demos.

Who is this for?

Solo founders, indie hackers, and freelancers shipping web apps with Docker who want staging without a platform team.

How does it give me a public URL?

SoloEnv runs your app and opens a secure tunnel to it, then prints a shareable https:// link. No domains to buy and no inbound ports to open.

Will it cost anything?

Pricing isn't set — that's part of what we're validating. The goal is something a solo dev can justify on day one, with no cloud account required to get started.
